UX case study

Kala

An app for discovering Indian art and heritage — research, flows, and screens for people who want to explore craft traditions without a museum guide.

UX researchMobileCulture
Flight log HK-01 · KALA
✈
  1. Departure

    People want to explore Indian art and craft, but existing apps read like museum catalogues — dense, chronological, built for scholars.

  2. Turbulence

    No content library to draw from, so every entry had to be researched and written. Early flows buried discovery behind search — people didn't know what to search for.

  3. Altitude

    Discovery should feel like wandering a gallery, not querying a database. Lead with story and region, not artist or era.

  4. Landing

    Reframed the app around browsable collections and a visual timeline — no search term required to reach a first artwork.

    3 taps to first artwork 5 collections shipped 8 test participants

UX case study

Porsche 911

A configurator and showcase experience for the 911 — treating spec, colour and trim choices as something to enjoy rather than a form to fill in.

InteractionAutomotiveWeb
Flight log HK-02 · 911
✈
  1. Departure

    Configurators treat spec selection as a form to complete. For a 911, that's backwards — the point is the fantasy of building your car.

  2. Turbulence

    Early versions front-loaded every option at once. Testers felt paralysed — nobody knew which choice actually mattered, so everyone hesitated.

  3. Altitude

    A configurator is a reveal, not a checklist. Give the car the room to be the hero and let choices unfold a few at a time.

  4. Landing

    Rebuilt as a scroll-driven reveal — the car stays centre stage while spec decisions step in one at a time, with live colour and trim previews.

    1 decision per screen 12 trim combos previewed full scroll-linked motion

Fintech case study

PayHK

A multi-currency wallet for freelancers who earn in one currency and live in another — send it, keep it, spend it.

FintechMobile UICase study
Flight log HK-03 · PAYHK
✈
  1. Departure

    Freelancers get paid in one currency and spend in another. Most wallets make them leave the home screen to deal with the gap.

  2. Turbulence

    The hard part wasn’t adding currencies, it was showing them without the home screen turning into a spreadsheet — balances, rates and fees all fighting for the same space.

  3. Altitude

    One featured currency up top, four actions underneath — send, convert, rates, receive. Everything else steps back until it’s needed.

  4. Landing

    A wallet that opens on a single balance and keeps every money move a tap away, built on three promises: multi-currency, instant transfers, smart rates.

    1 featured balance4 core actions3 product promises

Product UI

Crypto Wallet

Portfolio, transaction and swap screens for a crypto wallet — making volatile numbers readable at a glance without dramatising them.

FintechMobile UIData display
Flight log HK-04 · CRYPTO
✈
  1. Departure

    Crypto apps display every number at the same volume. Volatile and stable amounts look identical, so the screen reads as noise instead of information.

  2. Turbulence

    Tested red/green sparkline treatments early — they made routine fluctuations look like emergencies and pushed users to act when they shouldn't.

  3. Altitude

    A wallet should be calm. The portfolio number is the one thing that matters at a glance; everything else is context you opt into.

  4. Landing

    Portfolio value sits alone and steady, percentages are quiet by default, and sparklines only colour the extremes — never the middle.

    1 hero number muted colour palette 3 screens shipped

Brand identity

VOLT

Logo, type, colour and applications for VOLT — an identity built to hold up at small sizes and still feel charged at full scale.

IdentityLogoArt direction
Flight log HK-05 · VOLT
✈
  1. Departure

    VOLT needed a mark that could survive a 16px favicon and still feel electric at billboard scale — a single identity doing both jobs.

  2. Turbulence

    First wordmark leaned decorative — the lightning bolt looped through the O and vanished entirely below 24px. It looked great in the pitch deck and nowhere else.

  3. Altitude

    Voltage isn't decoration — it's a cut. The mark should feel like a live wire, not an illustration of one.

  4. Landing

    Rebuilt the wordmark around a single sharp slash through the V — legible as a favicon, unmistakable as a poster, and holding across colour, mono and reverse.

    16px minimum legible mark 3 colourways tested 1 cut, everywhere

UX case study

BASTION

AI-driven security intelligence for modern developers — one platform to detect threats, understand vulnerabilities and ship fixes without leaving the workflow.

UI/UXProduct designAI
Flight log HK-06 · BASTION
✈
  1. Departure

    Security tools bury developers in alerts, then send them somewhere else to understand and fix each one. The threat is found in one place and dealt with in three.

  2. Turbulence

    Alert fatigue. When too many alerts turn out to be false positives, developers stop trusting the ones that matter, and a dashboard full of red just becomes wallpaper.

  3. Altitude

    Earn trust before asking for action: rank by severity, show how confident the AI is, and put the fix in the same place as the finding.

  4. Landing

    A single dashboard that runs the whole loop — detect, triage, fix, ship — ending in a generated fix that is ready as a pull request.

    4 stages, detect to ship3 severity tiers

UX case study

CINERANK

A TV and cinema guide that sparks joy — exploring how ranked lists, social proof and fast discovery change what people choose to watch.

UX researchMobile app
Flight log HK-07 · CINERANK
✈
  1. Departure

    Guides list everything and help with nothing. Endless rows, no reason to pick one, and choosing takes longer than watching.

  2. Turbulence

    A ranking is a shortcut, but it can flatten taste, and social proof can drown out the film itself. The design had to use both without letting either do all the talking.

  3. Altitude

    Let the ranking decide and the film do the selling: a big number-one pick up front, rating and runtime at a glance, genre filters one tap away.

  4. Landing

    A mobile guide that lands on real-time rankings — discover, track and rate your favourite films without digging through menus.

    #1 pick up front

Brand & web

yuiop

A brand and site for a name hidden in every keyboard — a glowing script wordmark on a dark canvas that lets it shine.

BrandingWeb design
Flight log HK-08 · YUIOP
✈
  1. Departure

    A name that’s just five keys from the top row of a keyboard. It means nothing until someone tells you where to look.

  2. Turbulence

    A plain wordmark would read as a typo. The name is hard to say, easy to mistype, and easy to forget.

  3. Altitude

    Lean into the hiding. A script wordmark with offset colour layers, the line “hidden in every keyboard”, and the letters spelled out in the ticker — the name becomes the story.

  4. Landing

    A dark, glowing brand site — Home, Story, Vision, Team, Lab — where the wordmark is the hero and the colour does the talking.

    5 keys behind the name5 site sections
